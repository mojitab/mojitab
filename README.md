### 🚀 About Me

I am a **Growth Marketer & Sales Strategist** passionate about scaling revenue, designing high-converting customer acquisition funnels, and optimizing digital ad spend. I leverage data analytics to turn visitor traffic into loyal, paying customers.

- 🎯 **Sales & Lead Generation**: Crafting end-to-end multi-stage sales funnels, CRM pipelines, and automated lead nurturing systems.
- 📈 **Performance Advertising**: Managing and optimizing ROI-focused ad campaigns across Google Ads, Meta (Instagram/Facebook), and display networks.
- 🔍 **Data-Driven Tracking**: Building measurement frameworks using GA4, Google Tag Manager, and custom Looker Studio dashboards for actionable business insights.
- 💡 **Growth & CRO**: Conducting A/B testing, user journey mapping, and conversion rate optimization to maximize customer lifetime value (LTV).
